# Client questions template

This goes to the client, so write it for them: short, warm, plain English, no jargon, and none of the team's internal notes. Put the most important questions first. Number questions continuously across the themes so the client can reply by number. Give every question a suggested answer, because a client who is short of time can then just reply "all fine" and the project keeps moving.

Add a "why we're asking" line only where a question could be misread or seems odd out of context.

```markdown
# <Project name>: a few questions before we start

Hi <client contact's first name>,

We're planning the research now, and your answers to these will shape it. Partial answers and "not sure" are both useful. Under each question we've put what we'd suggest. If we haven't heard back by <date>, we'll go with those suggestions.

## <Theme, for example "What the research is for">

**1. <Question>**
Our suggestion: <recommended answer>.
Why we're asking: <one line, only if needed>.
Your answer:

**2. <Question>**
Our suggestion: <recommended answer>.
Your answer:

## <Next theme>

**3. <Question>**
...

## Anything else?

Is there anything else we should know before we start?
Your answer:
```

Leave the date as a placeholder, `<DATE>`, unless the user has given one.
