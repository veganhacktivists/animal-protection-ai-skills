# AI in Your Work

Explore where AI might help in a nonprofit role, department or organization through a one-to-one interview. Leave with a shortlist, a small experiment, a process improvement, or a reason to wait.

## Try it

Ask your agent:

> Read this folder's SKILL.md and interview me about possible uses of AI across my work. Explore a range of activities before narrowing. Ask one question at a time. I'd like a shortlist first.

Use “our department's work” or “our organization's work” for a wider scope. For a chosen task, ask “Help me work through [task]”. Keep the references folder with SKILL.md when installing. No connectors or accounts are required. Use fictional or permitted material.

## Verification

The local v0.2 interview guidance was tested on Codex in 19 simulated multi-turn conversations on 2026-11-03, covering broad and focused routes at all three scopes. All passed the declared criteria, with minor summary omissions, repetition and a final role ambiguity recorded. These synthetic tests do not establish usefulness, comfort or time savings with real nonprofit staff. This repository version retains those interview routes and generalizes the personal email-review wording.
