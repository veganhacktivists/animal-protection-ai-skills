# A small experiment

Use this only after the participant chooses to develop an idea into a trial.
A wide scan can finish with the shortlist in `opportunity-scan.md`; an experiment
is not a required output of every conversation.

Make this short enough to use. It can be a practice exercise, a proposed trial awaiting agreement, or an agreed trial. Label that status correctly. If no experiment fits, give the simpler next step instead.

## Suggested take-away

**The work:** The task, how it happens now, and the participant's role. Note whether this is one person's account or has been checked with others.

**What we want to improve:** One outcome in their words. State the current baseline if known; otherwise name the one thing to observe before comparing.

**What to try:** One specific change, on one occurrence or a small sample. Explain which part AI assists with and who remains responsible. A fictional practice task is a valid first step.

**Material and tools:** The allowed input, permitted tool if established, and anything to leave out. If permission is unknown, do not use live organizational data.

**Before starting:** The decision or agreement needed, who can make it if known, and any missing perspective. Write “not established” rather than inventing permission.

**Check the result:** Who checks it, against which source or criteria, and what would make it unusable. Include checking time in the effort. For factual drafting, look for invented or unsupported claims, omissions, and incorrect meaning.

**Limit the effort:** The time or number of attempts the participant is willing to spend. Record their limit, not an invented budget.

**When to try and review:** A natural occasion, followed by a simple comparison: was the result useful, was it better or easier overall, and what extra work did it create? Do not schedule anything automatically.

**Keep, change, or stop:** A practical success condition and a reason to stop, agreed with the participant. They can stop because the benefit does not justify the effort. A practice success does not authorize wider use.

Before returning the take-away, check the original units, numbers, roles and
scope. A limit on how many rows someone can check must remain a count limit;
distinguish an individual time cap from the combined effort. Confirm that
participation has been established and retain relevant reported reservations.

## Optional starter prompt

Customize this only when useful. It is a template, not permission to share a real source:

> Help me [specific task] using only the material below, which I am permitted to share. The result should [participant's quality criteria]. If information is missing or inconsistent, identify it rather than inventing an answer. Flag statements I need to check before using the result. I will review it before use.
>
> Material: [approved excerpt or clearly fictional practice input]

For an email-related task, draft a message for the participant to review. Never put automatic sending into the experiment or starter prompt.
